🎯 Screen 1: Single Bidder Index

Measures the percentage of contracts where only one bidder participated - a key indicator of potential collusion or market manipulation.

Warning Threshold: Sectors with single-bid rates above 30% require investigation. The overall rate of 21.3% is concerning for a public procurement market.

📊 Screen 2: HHI Market Concentration

The Herfindahl-Hirschman Index measures market concentration. Higher values indicate more concentrated (less competitive) markets.

Thresholds: HHI < 1500 = Competitive | HHI 1500-2500 = Moderately Concentrated | HHI > 2500 = Highly Concentrated

🔢 Screen 3: Benford's Law Test

Natural data follows a predictable distribution of first digits. Significant deviations (high χ² values) suggest potential price manipulation or bid rigging.

Interpretation: Chi-square values above 15.51 (α=0.05 with 8 degrees of freedom) indicate statistically significant deviation from expected distribution.
