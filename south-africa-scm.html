📚 About Synthetic Control Method

The Synthetic Control Method (SCM) creates a "synthetic" version of the treated unit (cement prices) by weighting control units (other construction materials) to match pre-intervention trends.

How it works: We train a model during the cartel period (2000-2009) to predict cement prices based on other construction input costs (Plumbing, Drainage, Ironmongery). After the cartel dissolves, we compare actual prices to what the model predicts.

Key Findings:

  • Perfect Model Fit: During the cartel period, the synthetic control matches actual prices almost exactly (Gap: -0.1%)
  • Post-Cartel Divergence: After 2009, actual prices fall significantly below the synthetic counterfactual
  • Consumer Dividend: Prices are 21-32% lower than they would have been under continued cartel pricing
  • Institutional Effect: This confirms the positive impact of antitrust enforcement on consumer welfare

Interpretation:

The green shaded area in the main chart represents consumer savings - the difference between what prices would have been (synthetic) and what they actually are (actual). This "institutional dividend" grows over time as competition continues to exert downward pressure on prices.
